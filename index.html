How I work

  1. 01

    Understand

    Understand the requirements, the goals, and who the site is actually for.

  2. 02

    Plan

    Break the requirement into pages, features and milestones before writing code.

  3. 03

    Design

    Plan the interface and user experience, usually in Figma, before it becomes a build.

  4. 04

    Build

    Develop the responsive, interactive experience with clean, organized code.

  5. 05

    Test

    Check functionality, responsiveness and usability across real screen sizes.

  6. 06

    Deliver

    Deploy and hand off the finished project, ready to use.
